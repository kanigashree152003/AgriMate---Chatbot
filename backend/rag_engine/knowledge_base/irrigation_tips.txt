Maintain 2–3 cm of standing water during the active tillering stage.

Drain fields before applying fertilizers and re-flood after one day.

Stop irrigation 10 days before harvest to ensure uniform grain ripening.

Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) method saves water and maintains yield — allow the field to dry until small cracks appear before re-irrigating.

Avoid stagnant water for long durations to reduce disease incidence.
