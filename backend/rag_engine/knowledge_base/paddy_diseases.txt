Rice blast is caused by the fungus Magnaporthe oryzae. Symptoms include diamond-shaped lesions on leaves. Use resistant varieties and avoid excess nitrogen fertilizers.

Bacterial leaf blight is caused by Xanthomonas oryzae. Symptoms are yellowing and wilting of leaves. Control by using clean seeds and resistant cultivars.

Sheath blight is a fungal disease that causes irregular lesions on the sheath. Apply Tricyclazole or Propiconazole-based fungicides.

Brown spot is caused by Bipolaris oryzae. Symptoms include small circular brown lesions. Use balanced fertilization and good field drainage.

Tungro virus causes stunted growth and yellow-orange leaf discoloration. Control vectors (green leafhoppers) and remove infected plants.
