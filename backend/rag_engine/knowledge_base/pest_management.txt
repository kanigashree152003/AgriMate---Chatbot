Stem borer causes dead hearts and whiteheads in paddy. Control by clipping seedlings and applying Carbofuran granules at 10 kg/ha.

Brown planthopper causes hopper burn and transmits viruses. Avoid excessive nitrogen, maintain proper spacing, and use Imidacloprid or Buprofezin sprays.

Leaf folder rolls leaves and feeds inside. Use Trichogramma egg parasitoids and spray Chlorantraniliprole.

Gall midge causes silver shoots in seedlings. Use resistant varieties and apply Neem oil or Carbofuran.

Armyworm and cutworm feed on leaves. Spray Lambda-cyhalothrin or Chlorpyrifos in the evening.
