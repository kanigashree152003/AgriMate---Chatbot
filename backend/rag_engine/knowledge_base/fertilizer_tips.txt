For paddy crops, apply 150 kg Nitrogen, 50 kg Phosphorus, and 50 kg Potassium per hectare in split doses.

At transplanting: Apply 50% Nitrogen and full dose of Phosphorus and Potassium.

At tillering: Apply 25% Nitrogen.

At panicle initiation: Apply the remaining 25% Nitrogen.

Zinc deficiency shows yellow leaves and stunted growth. Apply 25 kg Zinc Sulfate per hectare.

Always maintain proper irrigation and avoid waterlogging after fertilizer application.
