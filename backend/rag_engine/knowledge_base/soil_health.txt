Test soil every two seasons to monitor nutrient levels.

Apply organic manure or compost at 5–10 tons per hectare to improve soil fertility.

Use green manure crops like Sesbania or Sunn hemp before paddy planting.

Maintain soil pH between 6.0 and 7.5 for optimum nutrient availability.

Avoid continuous rice cultivation; rotate with legumes to restore soil nitrogen.

Biofertilizers such as Azospirillum and Phosphobacteria improve soil health naturally.
